# ESTIMATOR: point estimate AND bootstrap statistic are the RATIO OF SUMS
#   (total calls / total wall-time); mean-of-per-item-rates is Jensen-biased
#   upward by ~CV^2 of the item times (+40% measured on the heavy-tailed
#   cubeful Sage analyzer row), hence ratio-of-sums everywhere. Resampling is
#   over INDIVIDUAL items (bench_common.rate).
# WARMUP: every timed config is preceded by a fixed 3s (BENCH_WARMUP_S)
#   core-clock ramp via a cache-NEUTRAL scalar spin loop plus ~20 real calls
#   (weights/code only) — ramps the clock without pre-caching the pool (a
#   full-pool warmup inflated tiny-eval configs 2-7x). This matters: the
#   amd-pstate-epp governor needs ~2s to reach boost clock, so an unwarmed
#   process pays the ramp inside its timed region.
